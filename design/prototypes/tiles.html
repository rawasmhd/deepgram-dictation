Tiles

Windows' own Metro. Flat colour tiles on pure black or pure white, giant lowercase Segoe cropped at the edge, and no chrome at all. One teal owns every live state. The meter is a live tile that flips when the state changes.

Follows Windows mode. The desktop, the taskbar and the product change together.

The meter, live

Above the taskbar, 12 px up, never focusable. A teal tile with a red square as the recording mark, level squares that light with your voice, the timer, and the word "recording" cropped at the tile's edge. Press the buttons or let it run.

Every state of the meter

Each state is a tile of its own colour: teal recording, orange paused, cyan transcribing, teal notice, red error. The tile flips to change. Black type on the light tiles, white on red, so every word reads.

Settings

Opened at first run, from the tray, or with --setup. The heading runs off the window's edge, Metro style. The key field is a white box with black text in both modes. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
