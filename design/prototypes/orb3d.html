Orb 3D

A small dark glass sphere above the taskbar, lit from inside: red while you talk, amber when another window has focus, blue while the words arrive. One soft trace inside it moves with your voice. Only a notice adds a few words beside it.

The notice pill follows Windows mode. The sphere does not change.

Notes

  • The sphere is dark glass: a vertical gradient, a lit core in the state colour, a faint ring, a rim light on the upper left, one soft highlight, and a glow beneath. The trace is one smooth line, its amplitude from the last 40 level samples. The app can draw all of it pixel by pixel, as it draws the meter today.
  • The level bars, the timer and the words go. The tray icon keeps its state dot.
