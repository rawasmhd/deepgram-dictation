Orb 3D

A small dark glass sphere above the taskbar, lit from inside: red while you talk, amber when another window has focus, blue while the words arrive. Two ways the voice can show inside it: as a liquid whose level is your voice, or as rings that ripple out at each louder moment. Only a notice adds a few words beside it.

The notice pill follows Windows mode. The sphere does not change.

The orb, live

36 px sphere, 12 px above the taskbar, centred. Never focusable, never clickable. Press the buttons or let it run.

Every state

Recording, Paused, Transcribing, a notice and an error, on a dark and a light desktop. While transcribing, the liquid rests at half with a slow swell, or the rings keep a steady beat.

Sizes

28, 36 and 44 px, recording. The tray icon beside each is 16 px for scale.

Notes

  • The sphere is dark glass: a vertical gradient, a lit core in the state colour, a faint ring, a rim light on the upper left, one soft highlight, and a glow beneath. Liquid: the colour fills from the bottom to a level that follows the voice, with a two-wave surface and a paler back wave. Rings: one ring per rise in the level, growing and fading over 0.9 s. The app can draw all of it pixel by pixel, as it draws the meter today.
  • The level bars, the timer and the words go. The tray icon keeps its state dot.
  • Open: size (28 to 44), the amount of shading, and whether the waveform should keep a faint trace while paused.
