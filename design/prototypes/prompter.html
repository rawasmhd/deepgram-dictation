Prompter

A teleprompter. Black glass, large white type, and a cue marker that tells where you are. One theme: Windows light mode does not change it, so the meter reads the same over every app.

Changes the desktop and the taskbar only. The product stays black.

The meter, live

Above the taskbar, 12 px up, never focusable. The cue triangle is the recording mark; the level is a strip of white blocks; the timer is the only large thing on screen. Press the buttons or let it run.

Every state of the meter

Recording, Paused, Transcribing, an information notice and an error. Each has a mark, a colour and a word. The level keeps moving while Paused and stops while Transcribing, where the cue line sweeps instead.

Settings

Opened at first run, from the tray, or with --setup. The key field is the only standard control. Enter saves, Esc cancels.
