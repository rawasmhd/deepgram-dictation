Orb

One small disc above the taskbar. Red while you talk, amber when another window has focus, blue while the words arrive. A soft halo breathes with your voice. Only a notice grows a few words, then it shrinks back.

The notice pill follows Windows mode. The disc colours do not change.

The orb, live

14 px disc, 12 px above the taskbar, centred. Never focusable, never clickable. Press the buttons or let it run.

Every state

Recording, Paused, Transcribing, a notice and an error, on a dark and on a light desktop. The halo is drawn at a loud moment.

Sizes

The same orb at 10, 14 and 18 px, recording, so the size can be picked. The tray icon beside each is 16 px for scale.

What this changes and what it does not

  • The level bars, the timer and the words "Paused" and "Transcribing" go. The colour says it.
  • The tray icon keeps its state dot, in the same colours, so the state is also visible when the orb is hidden.
  • Notices keep their words. Without words a notice cannot say what went wrong.
  • Open questions: a timer on hover or not at all; the orb's position (centre, or above the tray icon); whether the halo should show the level at all.
