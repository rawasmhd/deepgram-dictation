Studio

A broadcast studio desk. A bakelite panel with engraved cream labels, a red ON AIR lamp that lights while you speak, and a VU needle that swings with your voice. Windows light mode is the same desk in cream enamel.

Changes the desktop, the taskbar and the meter: Studio follows Windows mode.

The meter, live

Above the taskbar, 12 px up, never focusable. The lamp is the recording mark; the needle is the level; the timer is engraved at the right. Press the buttons or let it run.

Every state of the meter

Recording, Paused, Transcribing, an information notice and an error. Each has a lamp colour, an engraved tag and a word. The needle keeps moving while Paused and rests at -20 while Transcribing, where the green lamp carries the state. Shown in both Windows modes.

Settings

Opened at first run, from the tray, or with --setup. The content sits in a bezel panel; the keys are bakelite, the Save key is brass. Enter saves, Esc cancels. Dark and light.
